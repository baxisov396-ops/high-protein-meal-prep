You need a PDF reader for the guide and a compatible spreadsheet app, such as Microsoft Excel or another app that can open XLSX files, for the tracker.

Yes. The menu ideas are flexible. You can swap meals, repeat favorites and adapt ingredients to your preferences, budget and availability.
